02 / LMS Solution

A moving-magnet logistics system that
drives carriers independently, with no cables.

Carrier transport is performed through the continuous operation of individual coils, while carrier position control is based on hall sensors at the entry point. Rather than a coil (motor), a magnet array is attached to and moves with the carrier — a structure that requires no separate external position-detection sensors.

LMS (Linear Motion System) Advantages
  • Carrier transport without cables, even inside a vacuum
  • Independent high-speed, high-precision position control of multiple carriers in high-temperature and special environments
  • Non-contact carrier position sensing using hall sensors — no separate encoder required
  • Supports not only straight tracks but also circular and oval tracks, elevators, and shift transfers
  • Maximizes productivity with no carrier waiting time, and enables logistics automation layout innovation through tracks of virtually any shape

02-2 / LMS Performance
PERFORMANCE
  • Wide payload and transport range: A broad spectrum covering everything from small parts to large loads of up to 1.5 tons.
  • High-speed transport: Moves large, heavy loads at speeds of up to 5 m/s, delivering excellent cost-effective productivity.
  • Reliability in extreme environments: In addition to standard NdFeB magnets (70°C), a high-temperature magnet option (SmCo magnet) supports operation up to 150°C.
  • Wide air gap: A generous 1–5mm air gap flexibly absorbs assembly tolerances and maintains stable running even with minor rail contamination or deformation.

03 / LMS Products

An LMS product lineup built for
high-temperature, high-vacuum, and other special environments.

CSCAM manufactures and supplies all components in-house, including the LMS coil, dedicated drive, and EtherCAT Master SW. PLC control via general-purpose ladder logic makes it easy to interconnect and integrate with other processes. The LMS coil incorporates a dual hall sensor for precise position control. The coil is designed in a compact size optimized for heat dissipation. Redundant EtherCAT communication cabling handles communication line breaks, and a proprietary communication kernel allows for customization.

PLC & HMI

  • Operator HMI screen design (e.g., Proface)
  • Up to 3 CPACK control units connectable to a single PLC
  • Ethernet (MX component) communication

CPACK : Control unit

  • Up to 255 drives connectable to a single CPACK control unit
  • Shared-memory communication support with the upper-level host (MXComponent)
  • Real-time EtherCAT communication with lower-level drives

LMS Package Drive

  • 2-channel encoder input (BiSS-C), EtherCAT CSV mode
  • Main: AC200~230V +10~-15% 50/60Hz
  • Package drive: up to 4 units of 2~3kW connectable
  • Individual LMS Drive (400W, 800W, 1.5kW, 2kW, 3kW) also supported

LMS Coil / Magnet

  • LMS coil series (widths 30 / 50 / 100, lengths 200 / 260 / 380)
  • Air gap: 1~5mm · Magnet pitch: 30mm
  • Skew angle: 19.5°(30T) · 6°(50T) · 4°(100T)
